# Domain 5 — Security Program Management & Oversight (≈20 %)

## Governance

- **Policies** (high-level, mandatory): AUP, information security, business
  continuity, disaster recovery, incident response, SDLC, change management.
- **Standards**: password, access control, physical security, encryption.
- **Procedures**: change management, onboarding/offboarding, playbooks.
- **Guidelines**: recommendations, not mandatory.
- External considerations: regulatory, legal, industry, local/regional,
  national, global. Monitoring & revision.
- Structures: boards, committees, government entities, centralised vs
  decentralised.
- Roles for systems & data: **owner** (accountable), **controller**
  (decides why/how personal data is processed), **processor** (processes on
  behalf of the controller), **custodian/steward** (day-to-day care).

## Risk management

- Identification → assessment (ad hoc, recurring, one-time, continuous) →
  analysis → register → treatment → reporting.
- **Qualitative** (high/medium/low, heat map) vs **quantitative**:
  - SLE = Asset value × Exposure factor
  - ARO = expected occurrences per year
  - **ALE = SLE × ARO**
- Risk register: key risk indicators, risk owners, risk threshold.
- Risk tolerance vs **risk appetite** (expansionary, conservative, neutral).
- Strategies: **transfer** (insurance), **accept** (exemption/exception),
  **avoid** (stop the activity), **mitigate** (controls).
- Business impact analysis: RTO, RPO, MTTR, MTBF.

## Third-party risk

Vendor assessment (pen testing, right-to-audit clause, evidence of internal
audits, independent assessments, supply chain analysis), vendor selection
(due diligence, conflict of interest), agreements: **SLA** (service levels),
**MOA/MOU** (intent), **MSA** (master terms), **WO/SOW** (specific work),
**NDA**, **BPA** (business partners). Vendor monitoring, questionnaires,
rules of engagement.

## Compliance

Internal/external reporting, consequences of non-compliance (fines,
sanctions, reputational damage, loss of licence, contractual impacts),
compliance monitoring (due diligence/care, attestation, automation).
**Privacy**: legal implications, data subject, controller vs processor,
ownership, data inventory & retention, **right to be forgotten** (GDPR).
In France: CNIL is the data-protection authority; ANSSI is the national
cybersecurity agency; NIS2 extends obligations to more sectors.

## Audits & assessments

Attestation, internal (compliance, audit committee, self-assessment),
external (regulatory, examinations, independent third party).
**Penetration testing**: physical, offensive (red), defensive (blue),
integrated (purple); known environment (white box), partially known (grey),
unknown (black box); reconnaissance passive vs active.

## Security awareness

Phishing campaigns & reporting, recognising anomalous behaviour (risky,
unexpected, unintentional), user guidance (policy handbooks, situational
awareness, insider threat, password management, removable media, social
engineering, operational security, hybrid/remote work), reporting and
monitoring (initial, recurring), development & execution.
