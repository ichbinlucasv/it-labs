# Ticket

Shorter than the full template in
[helpdesk/01](../../helpdesk/01-ticket-writing/template.md). Use that one
when the ticket will be audited. Use this one while I am practising speed.

```text
ID:
When opened (local):
Who asked (name, team, how they contacted):
Asset (name, OS):

Their words (quote):

Since when:
How many people:
Can they work?   yes / degraded / no

What I tried:
- [time]  ->
- [time]  ->

Exact error (paste):

Workaround, if they are waiting:

Next owner (me / 2nd line / vendor):
What that person needs from me (one paragraph, no "see above"):

Closed:
Cause:
Fix:
User confirmed?  yes / no / left a message
```

Do not put the new password in the ticket. Say "reset, user must change at
next logon" or "temporary secret given by phone, not stored here".
